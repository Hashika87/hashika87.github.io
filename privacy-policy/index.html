1. Introduction

HASIBTT (PVT) LTD respects the privacy of users of BizNative ERP. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, stored, used and protected when you use the BizNative ERP application and related services.

BizNative ERP is designed to help individuals and businesses manage inventory, sales, customers, expenses, employees, reports, POS operations and other business activities.

2. Information Stored in BizNative ERP

Depending on the features used by the user, BizNative ERP may store information such as:

  • Business and company information
  • Product and inventory information
  • Purchase and sales information
  • Customer information
  • Supplier information
  • Employee and staff information
  • Expenses and financial records
  • POS and transaction information
  • Reports and business analytics information
  • Other information voluntarily entered by the user

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the appropriate rights and permissions to enter and manage information relating to other individuals or organizations.

3. Local Data Storage

BizNative ERP may store business information locally on the user's device to provide offline functionality, improve performance and allow the application to continue operating when an internet connection is unavailable.

Local application data remains under the user's control through the application and the device on which it is installed.

4. Google Account and Google Drive

BizNative ERP may provide an optional feature that allows users to connect their Google Account and use their own Google Drive for backup, synchronization and access to BizNative ERP business data.

```

Important: Connecting Google Drive is optional. Users may continue using BizNative ERP without connecting a Google Account where the application functionality permits.

```

When a user chooses to connect Google Drive, BizNative ERP uses Google's authentication services to authenticate the selected Google Account.

Where the application uses the Google Drive drive.file permission, BizNative ERP is permitted to access files created or opened by the application according to Google's authorization model.

BizNative ERP does not require unrestricted access to the user's entire Google Drive for this functionality.

Google Account authentication and Google Drive services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's own terms and privacy policies.

5. Google Sign-In

Users may choose to sign in or connect services using a Google Account. Google authentication is handled through Google's authentication infrastructure.

BizNative ERP may receive basic account information necessary to identify the authenticated user and provide the requested service.

BizNative ERP does not receive or store the user's Google Account password.

6. How We Use Information

Information handled by BizNative ERP may be used to:

  • Provide business management and ERP functionality
  • Provide POS and sales management functionality
  • Manage inventory and stock information
  • Generate business reports and analytics
  • Provide optional backup and synchronization features
  • Improve application performance and reliability
  • Maintain application security
  • Detect and prevent misuse
  • Develop and improve future BizNative products and services

7. Business Data Ownership

Business information entered by a user into BizNative ERP remains under the user's or business owner's control, subject to the applicable service and storage arrangements.

HASIBTT (PVT) LTD does not claim ownership of a user's business records merely because the user stores or manages those records using BizNative ERP.

8. Data Sharing

HASIBTT (PVT) LTD does not sell, rent or trade users' personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed or disclosed where reasonably necessary to provide requested services, operate third-party integrations, comply with legal obligations, protect the security of the service, or prevent fraud and misuse.

9. Third-Party Services

BizNative ERP may use trusted third-party services to provide application functionality, authentication, analytics, cloud integration or advertising.

These may include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Sign-In
  • Google Drive API
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase or other analytics/crash-reporting services where enabled

These third-party services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Advertising

BizNative ERP may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may use device or advertising-related information to provide, measure and improve advertisements, subject to their own policies and applicable laws.

11. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Where analytics or crash-reporting services are enabled, limited technical information may be collected to help us understand application performance, identify errors and improve reliability.

Such information may include application version, device type, operating-system information, crash information and other technical diagnostic information.

12. Data Security

HASIBTT (PVT) LTD implements reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. User Control and Data Deletion

Users may stop using BizNative ERP at any time.

Users may also manage or delete their locally stored application information using the application's available features or the relevant device controls.

Where information is stored in the user's own Google Drive, the user may manage or delete those files through Google Drive, subject to Google's services and policies.

If you require assistance regarding information handled by HASIBTT (PVT) LTD, you may contact us using the details below.
